# Festive sale: demo script

The same story as `feeds/sale-demo.json`, about 45 seconds. Times are from the feed (`t`).
Each scene says where the camera sits in the office and what the Telegram group shows.

## 0:00 War Room: the ask
👑 **Founder:** "Plan our festive sale. 30% off, 5 days, ₹5L marketing budget."

🎯 **Sale Captain** starts working, then assigns: Analyst forecasts first, then Ads, Creators, Ops
and Design. Finance checks last.

## 0:04 Analytics: the forecast
📊 **Analyst** pulls past sales. Summer Sale 2026 gave a 4x uplift, 6x on the Glow Kit.

Forecast for 5 days:
- SKU01 Vitamin C Serum: 800
- SKU02 Sunscreen SPF 50: 1,400
- SKU03 Hydrating Moisturiser: 600
- SKU04 Kesar Face Oil: 240
- SKU05 Glow Kit: 300

Revenue ≈ ₹20.4L.

## 0:10 Every department at once
🎯 Captain hands out work in each group. Ads, Creators, Ops and Design all start working
(desks light up together).

📣 **Ads Lead:** Meta plan at ₹3L. Advantage+ Shopping ₹1.2L, Retargeting ₹0.9L, Lookalike 1% ₹0.9L.
Broad prospecting is cut (ROAS 1.6).

⭐ **Creators Lead:** top 4 by reach. Riya Glows, SkinBySana, The Derm Diaries, Meera Minimal.
Total fee ₹3.55L.

📦 **Ops Lead:** Delhi is short. SKU01 needs 320 and has 250. SKU05 needs 120 and has 80.
Books transfers Mumbai → Delhi: 70 × SKU01 and 40 × SKU05, arriving in 2 days.

🎨 **Designer:** pins three banners to the gallery: hero (`/gallery/hero.html`),
serum (`/gallery/serum.html`) and Glow Kit (`/gallery/glowkit.html`).

## 0:25 Finance: the pushback
💰 **Finance Lead** runs the P&L. Contribution before marketing ≈ ₹6.29L, so at ₹5L marketing
EBITDA would be +₹1.29L.

But marketing is ₹7.05L against a ₹5L budget. EBITDA −₹0.76L ❌.
"@Creators cut ₹2.05L." (EBITDA meter drops into the red.)

## 0:30 Creators: the fix
⭐ **Creators Lead** re-ranks by engagement and past ROAS. GlowWithAnu, Desi Skin Desk,
Kavya Care, Tanvi Tries. Total ₹1.18L.

💰 **Finance:** marketing ₹4.68L, EBITDA +₹1.61L ✅ Finance approved. (Meter swings green.)

## 0:37 War Room: sign-off
🎯 **Captain** posts the final plan: revenue ≈ ₹20.4L, Ads ₹3L, Creators ₹1.18L,
Delhi restocked, 3 banners live, EBITDA +₹1.61L. "@Founder approve?"

👑 **Founder:** "Looks great." Then "Approved. Ship it!" 🚀 Everyone celebrates.
